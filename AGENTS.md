# Project documentation and evidence

## Maintain the project overview

README.md is the public introduction to the current project. When a meaningful experiment, design revision, validation result or robotics milestone changes the project, update its narrative in the same change as the relevant evidence and logs. Before finishing, check whether the research question, investigation sequence, result table, design rationale, limitations and next research step still describe the current state. A small observation can remain in the detailed logs until it changes the overview's conclusions.

Revise the existing narrative to connect questions, observations, controlled comparisons and decisions. Avoid an ever-growing chronological announcement list or a permanently frozen kickoff description. Explain what changed and what the evidence supports. Keep detailed history in the experiment and contribution logs.

## Ground every claim

- Link numerical claims to the exact dataset, protocol and source revision or validation manifest. Include cohort, budget, denominator and relevant implementation variant.
- Keep historical raw results and manifests immutable; use new artifact names for new runs. Do not silently replace a published baseline.
- Distinguish hypotheses, completed checks, guided user reports and future plans. Do not promote a chat-reported count to an independently verified dataset.
- Keep AI authorship/operation separate from the student's actual design, edits, reasoning and execution. Public writing must not imply unsupported first-person authorship or discovery.
- Keep simulation claims separate from physical measurements and examined-node counts separate from runtime or energy. Scope improvements to the tested conditions.
- Keep public documents focused on engineering questions, methods, decisions, evidence, contributions and limitations. Exclude unrelated personal background, external positioning goals, private document links and nontechnical deadline planning.

## Working checks

Inspect the worktree before editing and preserve unrelated local changes. For documentation changes, verify links, source-backed figures and obsolete references. For code or methods changes, run the relevant checks and record their actual outcome before updating claims. Stage only the intended files.

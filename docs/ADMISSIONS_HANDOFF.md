# Early/RD EC evidence protocol

## Official Common App constraints
- 50 characters for Position/Leadership, 100 for Organization, 150 for Activities Details (see https://www.commonapp.org/static/f133e7d8d4aa22598e58fdafd14c0e4c/Resource_FY_Activities_ENG_2025.06.25.pdf).
- Common App applicant affirmations require own work, factual truth and honest presentation; submitted applications cannot be changed directly: https://www.commonapp.org/application-affirmations/ . Contact each college to give permitted substantive updates.
- The user's existing EC draft already fills 10 rows. Do NOT automatically add a new eleventh; compare incremental value vs weak/repetitive listed entries, and consider including the work under Physics/Coding Club if it is only a 3-day launch.

## Early claim matrix
| Observed status by date | What may be said | What may NOT be said |
|---|---|---|
| Mentor/AI only made scaffold | Exploring robot planning benchmarks with assistance, if student truly started | I independently built algorithms / I developed a deployed robot |
| Student implemented/documented own feature and ran tests | Name that exact feature + number of tests, scope as synthetic simulations | Physically tested robot, reduced collisions, served users |
| Student ran reproducible paired trials and interpreted results | Exact algorithms, test conditions and measured trade-offs, accurately qualified | Invented gains, peer-reviewed publication, physical validation |
| RD student built hardware and ran studies | Matched actual recorded runs and quantified differences | Guessed percentages or unverified hardware claims |

## Candidate Early lines — do NOT submit until checked
**Position:** `Student Researcher & Developer` (under 50 characters).
**Organization:** `RoboMetrics | Independent Engineering R&D` (under 100).
**150-character candidate, conditional upon student code and tests:**
> Built a browser-based robot path-planning benchmark; compared A* and greedy search on seeded grid maps and documented trade-offs.

**More conservative if only research launch is real:**
> Began testing robot path-planning algorithms in synthetic grids; studied route efficiency and computation limits for future robotics research.

Neither version claims physical robot testing, and neither should be used unless the underlying student action occurred. Student authorship and AI assistance must remain defensible; if teacher/AI provided nearly all code, describe independent work much more narrowly.

## RD evidence hierarchy
1. Actual student-owned commits/decision logs with reviewable reasoning.
2. Reproducible raw experimental data, complete configuration and analysis script.
3. Original hardware or validated simulator methods and counterexamples.
4. Engineering report with hypotheses, measured uncertainty, limitations and negative results.
5. Demo and feedback corroboration where available.
6. Then 150-character application claim; not the reverse.

## Admissions positioning
- Existing **PC Lab penalty website**: systems design and community service, co-developed with teacher/friend per essay draft (completion not yet independently verified).
- **FlowBench**: queueing capacity / fairness of timestamped attendance (planning phase).
- **RoboMetrics**: route-planning efficiency under computational constraints; RD hardware sim-to-real validation (future planned).
- **WIS**: 2024 robotics and mobility research/presentation, not robot construction.
Avoid presenting small parallel prototypes as multiple mature standalone accomplishments. A coherent long-term R&D story may fit better in one application activity and supplementary project portfolio, depending on demonstrated outputs.
